# Learner Kit

Learner Kit supports adult self-learners and university students through independently usable tutoring skills.

## Language

**Standalone skill**:
A tutoring capability a learner can use directly with the relevant goal or material, without completing another activity or supplying a learner profile.
_Avoid_: Required workflow stage

**Supplied material**:
Notes, problems, excerpts, or other learning content the learner provides as the basis for an activity. Its presence does not establish that its claims are correct.

**Generated exercise**:
A practice or retrieval task created for the learner rather than taken from an identified source.

**Learner attempt**:
An answer or piece of reasoning the learner actually supplies for a task. A requested solution or unanswered question is not an attempt.

**Coached retry**:
A further attempt at a task after the learner has received help. It remains distinct from the initial attempt.

**Coverage depth**:
How thoroughly a recall quiz covers the material: Quick (main ideas), Standard (plus important details) or Deep (thorough, with follow-ups). It sets coverage and length, not difficulty.

**Question slot**:
One of the fixed number of questions announced before a recall quiz starts. A slot is resolved when the learner's answer has had feedback, or the learner said "don't remember", skipped, or asked for the answer. Hints and clarifications stay within the slot.

**Session recap**:
A short summary of material covered, help needed, and possible next steps, based on the available conversation. A supplied recap is learner-provided context rather than verified learning history.
